## 2. What Constitutes “Legal Responsibility”?

Legal responsibility is usually divided into two ideas:

- Liability: who pays for the damage
- Accountability: who made the decision

Law assumes intent, judgment, and responsibility belong to humans.

### Simple Example

An AI breach-detection tool blocks legitimate traffic.
A business loses revenue.

Was this:
- A software bug
- A configuration mistake
- Poor oversight

Or was it “AI’s fault”?

### Responsibility Comparison

| Responsibility | Human Expert | AI System | Notes |
|---------------|-------------|-----------|------|
| Intent | Yes | No | AI cannot form intent |
| Negligence | Yes | Indirect | Depends on design |
| Damage Cost | Yes | Indirect | Falls on organization |
| Legal Standing | Yes | No | AI is not a legal person |

AI lacks legal identity.
